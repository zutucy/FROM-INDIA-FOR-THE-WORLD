## Scope

The curriculum covers computing history, hardware, operating systems, programming, networking, frontend/backend engineering, databases, storage/media, cloud/DevOps, cybersecurity, data/AI, distributed systems, product/business, global regulation, trust and safety, testing, company operations and a production capstone. Social platforms are one case study among many; this is not a social-media-only guide.

## How to use the bundle

1. Read the repository root `QUESTION.md` to choose a question that interests you.
2. Read `handbook/00_START_HERE_AND_MASTER_MAP.md` for the full curriculum map.
3. Read the numbered parts in order, or follow the chapter links in `QUESTION.md`.
4. Use the Hinglish glossary and gap register as companions.
5. Build practical labs only on systems you own or are explicitly authorised to test.
6. Keep a personal learning log: what you understood, what you built, what failed and how you verified the fix.

## Limitations

This is an educational handbook, not a certification, official standard, legal opinion, security audit, penetration test, privacy impact assessment, tax opinion, financial forecast or guarantee of production readiness. No handbook can guarantee that a product is secure, legally compliant, scalable or commercially successful. Production readiness requires product-specific requirements, threat modelling, professional code review, security testing, privacy/legal review, accessibility testing, load testing, backup restoration and operational ownership.
