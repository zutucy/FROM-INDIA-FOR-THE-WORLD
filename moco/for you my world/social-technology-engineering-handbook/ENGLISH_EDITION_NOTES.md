## What was translated

The repository was compared file by file before translation. The translation work focused on material that was written in Hinglish:

- Part 01 — History of the Internet and the World Wide Web.
- Part 02 — History of Social Networks and Social Platforms.
- Part 03 — Product Strategy and the Complete Build Roadmap.
- Parts 04A–04C — Computer Hardware; Software and Operating Systems; Programming Languages and Developer Tools.
- Parts 05A–05D — Networking Foundations; IP Addressing/DNS/Routing; TCP/UDP/QUIC/HTTP/TLS; Network Security and Troubleshooting.
- The former Hinglish glossary and study guide, now titled `TECHNICAL_GLOSSARY_AND_STUDY_GUIDE.md`.

The translated chapters retain their original learning order, major topics, examples, tables, command snippets, exercises, self-check questions and reference lists. Wording has been edited into clear professional English for a broad international audience.

## What was preserved without rewriting

Parts 06–18, the 66-question index, the existing English repository policies, the licensing documents, publishing guide, attribution material and other supporting files were already written in English. They were retained rather than translated again. Only links and language references were updated where needed to point to the English glossary and English-edition metadata.

## Accuracy and translation note

A translation can clarify language but cannot make every historical claim, software release, price or legal statement permanently current. The source/version register is a dated snapshot. Before using a technical recommendation in production or relying on a legal statement, verify it against current official documentation and qualified professional advice where appropriate.

The English edition is a separate publication package. The original Hinglish edition is not modified by this release.
