# Community Code of Conduct

This repository is a learning space. Everyone participating in issues, discussions or other project spaces is expected to help keep it respectful, constructive and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient and welcoming to beginners.
- Critique technical ideas and evidence, not people.
- Explain disagreements with sources and clear reasoning.
- Accept that readers may have different experience levels, languages and backgrounds.
- Give credit to original authors, maintainers and sources.
- Keep discussions focused on learning, corrections and useful improvements.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, insults or discriminatory conduct.
- Doxxing, publishing private information or sharing secrets.
- Spam, scams, impersonation or misleading claims of endorsement.
- Encouraging unauthorised access, harm or unsafe testing.
- Plagiarism or knowingly misrepresenting another person's work as your own.

## Moderation

The project maintainer may edit, hide or close content that violates these expectations and may block repeat offenders, subject to GitHub's platform processes. This code of conduct does not create a service-level guarantee or replace GitHub's terms and policies.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's repository reporting tools or contact the maintainer through the repository's available channels. Do not post private personal information or active exploit details in a public issue.
