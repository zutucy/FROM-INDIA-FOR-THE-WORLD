## Start With Questions

Do not begin by memorising technical terms.

Start with questions:

- How does the Internet actually work?
- Who invented the technologies we use every day?
- How does a website communicate with a server?
- How do applications store millions of records?
- How do hackers find vulnerabilities?
- How do large technology companies operate across countries?

The `QUESTION.md` file inside the editions connects learning questions to the relevant chapters.

## Learning Philosophy

Every subject should explain:

1. Its history and origin.
2. Why it was created.
3. Who contributed to its development.
4. How it works internally.
5. Where it is used in real-world systems.
6. Which alternatives exist.
7. Its limitations, risks and trade-offs.
8. How to experiment and learn safely.

## Who Is This For?

Students, independent learners, developers, aspiring engineers, technology enthusiasts, and anyone curious about how modern technology works.

You do not need to be an expert to begin.
